## Cross-PFT Conflicts

- CRITICAL cross-PFT P competition: The Case #1385 fingerprint reveals that PFT10 rescue occurs primarily through REDUCING PFT9 P demand (stoich_phos_leaf_9 from upper bound 0.00428 to lower bound 0.00210) and PFT9 microbial competition (microb_bio_9 from 468.6 to 140.0), NOT through PFT10-specific parameter changes. This means PFT10 recovery and PFT9 target maintenance are in structural conflict — if PFT9 stoich_phos is reduced to enable PFT10 recovery, PFT9 tissue P:C will change which may affect PFT9 leaf quality and potentially biomass allocation. Need to verify PFT9 targets are preserved after stoich_phos_leaf_9 reduction.
- PFT7 leaf overshoot vs P access conflict: PFT7_leaf is currently OVERESTIMATED (31.0 vs 24.55 gC/m², +26%). Increasing PFT7 P access (via microb_bio_7 reduction) would WORSEN this overshoot by increasing leaf biomass further. Conversely, turnover_leaf_7 reduction (Priority 5) to fix the overshoot may reduce litter input and degrade PFT7_fineroot (currently 150.7 vs 174.3, marginally satisfying ±20%). These two PFT7 targets require opposing interventions.
- Shared ECA microbial biomass: microb_bio_7 and microb_bio_9 both compete with all three PFTs for the shared soil P pool. Reducing microb_bio_9 benefits ALL PFTs (including PFT7 and PFT10), not just PFT9. This creates a non-linear interaction where microb_bio_9 reduction simultaneously helps PFT10 recovery AND potentially pushes PFT7_leaf further above target. Parameter changes must be designed as a coordinated intervention, not individual adjustments.
- phen_gddthresh_c shared phenology: Morris sensitivity analysis shows phen_gddthresh_c is rank #1 for PFT7 abg biomass (μ*=0.791), PFT9 abg biomass (μ*=0.440), and PFT10 abg biomass (μ*=0.144) simultaneously. Any change to this parameter would propagate competing effects across all three PFTs. In Case #86, phen_gddthresh_c=-0.01074. This parameter is not recommended for adjustment in the current cycle due to its cross-PFT sensitivity — it should be handled via ensemble-level optimization.
- nitr_store_ratio_10 and phos_store_ratio_10 both at upper bound (5.0) in Case #86: Simultaneously reducing both creates a 'double storage reduction' that maximizes structural allocation but may compromise plant resilience to seasonal N/P fluctuations in Arctic conditions. Monitor stress indicators after both reductions.
