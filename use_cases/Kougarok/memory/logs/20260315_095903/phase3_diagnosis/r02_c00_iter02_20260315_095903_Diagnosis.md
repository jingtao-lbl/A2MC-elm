No parameter combination within current bounds can bridge this 5-order-of-magnitude gap — confirmed by 86.6% zero-target failure rate and stoich_phos hypothesis rejection.

PFT10 HYDRAULIC FAILURE MORTALITY (SECONDARY, NEW): Mortality analysis shows PFT10 hydraulic failure causes 92% of deaths (dominant_cause='hydraulic'). mort_hf_sm_threshold_10 is at lower bound (1e-08, verified in Case #322 edge analysis). This is mechanistically INDEPENDENT of P starvation and explains why PFT10 biomass is near zero even compared to what little P is available.
3. EXTREME L2FR CREATING ROOT CARBON TRAP (TERTIARY): Case #322 has l2fr_ini_9=18.31 (at upper bound) and l2fr_ini_10=9.88 (at upper bound). High L2FR drives excessive fine root carbon allocation, depleting carbon available for leaf growth. The L2FR carbon limitation test showed confidence 0.60 that high L2FR reduces PFT9 leaves by 63%. Combined with P starvation, this creates a carbon-nutrient double constraint on leaf biomass.
4. WITHIN-ENSEMBLE PARAMETER SPACE IS OUTSIDE THE FEASIBLE REGION (CONFIRMED): 28.4% of Case #322 parameters are at sampling bounds. The stoich_phos hypothesis rejection confirms the parameter space does not bracket the solution. The model cannot simultaneously satisfy nutrient cycling constraints (which require either protocol-level P supplementation or drastically expanded parameter ranges) using within-bounds parameter tuning.
5. GRAMINOID ROOT PERSISTENCE NOT A DRIVER (RULED OUT): test_graminoid_root_persistence returned confidence 0.30 with turnover-froot correlation r=-0.008 (p=0.570). Fine root turnover is NOT controlling PFT10 fine root biomass — hydraulic failure mortality is the dominant mechanism eliminating plants before they can accumulate fine root biomass.

## Parameter Recommendations

### fates_mort_hf_sm_threshold (mort_hf_sm_threshold_10) (Priority: 1)
- **Direction:** increase significantly — explore range 1e-06 to 1e-04 for PFT10; current upper bound is 1.44e-06 which is still very low. Consider expanding upper bound to 1e-04.
- **Issue:** At lower bound (1e-08) in Case #322. Hydraulic failure causes 92% of PFT10 mortality. The threshold at 1e-08 means any soil moisture below this level triggers hydraulic failure death — likely too sensitive for Arctic graminoids that tolerate saturated/waterlogged soils. PFT10 is dying from hydraulic failure before P limitation can even become the binding constraint.

### fates_mort_scalar_hydrfailure (mort_scalar_hydrfailure_10) (Priority: 2)
- **Direction:** decrease toward lower bound 0.05 for PFT10 specifically. Combined with raising mort_hf_sm_threshold_10, this creates a two-lever reduction in hydraulic mortality.
- **Issue:** Case #322 has mort_scalar_hydrfailure_10=0.410 (mid-range). Given hydraulic failure causes 92% of PFT10 mortality, reducing this scalar would directly reduce PFT10 mortality rate and allow biomass accumulation.

### fates_allom_l2fr (l2fr_ini_9 and l2fr_ini_10) (Priority: 3)
- **Direction:** decrease both parameters significantly. For PFT9, explore range 2-8 (well below current lower bound of 18.31 upper). For PFT10, explore range 1-5 (current range 1.11-9.88). This requires expanding the lower bound of the sampling range for PFT9.
- **Issue:** Case #322 has l2fr_ini_9=18.31 (at upper bound) and l2fr_ini_10=9.88 (at upper bound). These extreme values drive massive fine root carbon allocation, starving leaf C pools. Best case for PFT9 leaf is partially achieved (101.8 vs 124.7) but the L2FR carbon limitation analysis shows high L2FR reduces PFT9 leaves by 63%.

### fates_cnp_vmax_p (vmax_p_10 specifically) (Priority: 4)

However, since stoich_phos reduction was rejected as a fix within ensemble bounds, vmax_p increases may slightly help but cannot overcome the protocol-level P wall.

- **Current:** `NONE` → **Proposed:** `ALL`
- **Rationale:** The stoich_phos skip-test REJECTION (confidence 0.11) provides definitive evidence that parameter-level fixes are insufficient. Enabling SUPLPHOS=ALL in RGSP phase would allow vegetation to equilibrate to observed biomass levels before transitioning to P-limited conditions in TRANS. This is the most likely path to escaping the degenerate P-starved state.
- **Requires re-spinup:** Yes

### A2MC_TRANS_SUPLPHOS (Priority: 2)
- **Current:** `NONE` → **Proposed:** `PARTIAL (or implement a gradual P withdrawal ramp)`
- **Rationale:** Even if RGSP is fixed, the abrupt P withdrawal in TRANS will cause vegetation collapse as observed in current results. A partial supplementation in TRANS (e.g., 50% of demand met by supplement) or a ramp-down over 20-30 years would allow a more physiologically realistic transition. If PARTIAL is not available as a setting, NONE may be acceptable in TRANS only if RGSP=ALL is implemented and the spinup is long enough (>50 years) for soil P pools to equilibrate.
- **Requires re-spinup:** Yes

### A2MC_ADSP_NYEARS_AD_CARBON_ONLY (Priority: 3)
- **Current:** `30` → **Proposed:** `50`
- **Rationale:** The carbon balance analysis shows GPP=0 for year 1901, suggesting the vegetation has not established a viable carbon economy before nutrient cycling is introduced. Extending the carbon-only AD spinup to 50 years would allow larger initial biomass pools that are more resilient to the P transition shock. This is a secondary recommendation pending the primary SUPLPHOS protocol fix.
- **Requires re-spinup:** Yes
