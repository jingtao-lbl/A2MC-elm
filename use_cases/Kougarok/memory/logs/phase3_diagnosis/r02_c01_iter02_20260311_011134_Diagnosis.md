## Cross-PFT Conflicts

- CONFLICT 1 — ECA zero-sum competition: vmax_p parameters for PFT7, PFT9, PFT10 interact multiplicatively in ECA competition. Increasing PFT7 vmax_p alone worsens PFT9/PFT10 suppression. ALL THREE vmax_p ranges must be expanded proportionally to maintain competitive balance. The current extreme ratio (vmax_p_7/vmax_p_10 = 572,000x in Case #322) drives 73.4% PFT7 dominance — this ratio must be reduced to <10x for any PFT10 viability.
- CONFLICT 2 — l2fr and P demand coupling: l2fr_ini parameters directly inflate P demand through increased root biomass. Expanding vmax_p without contracting l2fr_ini will partially offset the gain. Coordinated reduction of l2fr_ini_9 from [0.01, 18.31] to [1.0, 6.0] and l2fr_ini_10 from [1.1, 9.9] to [2.0, 6.0] is required alongside vmax_p expansion.
- CONFLICT 3 — PFT7 leaf overestimate vs PFT7 fineroot underestimate: Case #322 shows PFT7_leaf=32.5 (+32% over target 24.55) while PFT7_froot=62.3 (-64% below target 174.25). Reducing l2fr for PFT9/PFT10 while keeping PFT7 l2fr low (0.85 in Case #322) creates competing pressures: PFT7 allocation is leaf-biased when roots need investment. PFT7 l2fr_ini_7=0.85 may be too LOW — needs to INCREASE to shift C allocation toward PFT7 fineroot. This is OPPOSITE to the PFT9 recommendation.
- CONFLICT 4 — Stoichiometry and P demand interaction: stoich_phos_leaf_7=0.00181 and stoich_phos_fineroot_7=0.00109 in Case #322 determine target P concentration in plant tissues. Higher stoichiometry → higher P demand. These are near-default values but interact with vmax_p to determine whether supply can meet demand. If vmax_p is expanded dramatically, stoichiometry targets become the binding constraint.
- CONFLICT 5 — microb_bio_7 affects N and P simultaneously: Reducing microb_bio_7 from 600 to 280-400 will reduce both microbial P competition AND microbial N immobilization. This is beneficial for plant P access but may also reduce N mineralization (as microbial N becomes less immobilized). Monitor N limitation response after microb_bio_7 reduction.
