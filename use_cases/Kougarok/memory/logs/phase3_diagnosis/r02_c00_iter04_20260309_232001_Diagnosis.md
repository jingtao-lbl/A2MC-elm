- **Direction:** decrease by 100-1000x (from 0.00025 to range 2.5e-07 to 2.5e-06)
- **Issue:** Case #322 has vmax_nh4_7=0.00025 at the absolute upper bound of sampling range [2.5e-10, 0.00025]. This creates an astronomically high N/P demand in ECA: demand = vmax × fnrt_C. PFT7 alone drives 192,556 g/m²/yr of total system P demand (54% of 358,121). Reducing to 2.5e-07 (1000x reduction, back toward physical midpoint of the log-scale range) brings PFT7 N demand to a biologically plausible range without eliminating uptake capacity.

### vmax_no3_9 (fates_cnp_vmax_no3, PFT9) (Priority: 1)
- **Direction:** decrease by 100x (from 0.00025 to ~2.5e-06)
- **Issue:** Case #322 has vmax_no3_9=0.00025 at upper bound [2.5e-10, 0.00025]. PFT9 drives 165,550 g/m²/yr of total P demand (46% of total). ## Cross-PFT Conflicts

- VMAX REDUCTION CONFLICT: Reducing vmax_nh4_7 and vmax_no3_9 will reduce total system P demand, benefiting PFT10. However, if the reduction is too aggressive, PFT7 and PFT9 may become N-limited and lose their currently near-target biomass values. The correct target range for total system P demand is ~5-50 g/m²/yr (vs current 358,121), achievable with 100x vmax reduction while maintaining biological realism.
- L2FR REBALANCING CONFLICT: Increasing l2fr_ini_7 (more roots for PFT7) while decreasing l2fr_ini_9 (fewer roots for PFT9) creates opposing carbon allocation changes. The interaction risk: PFT7 with more roots increases PFT7 P demand via ECA (demand = vmax × fnrt_C), potentially competing more aggressively with PFT10 for P. This must be paired with vmax reduction for PFT7 to avoid creating a new P competition bottleneck.
- ECA COMPETITIVE EXCLUSION AFTER VMAX REDUCTION: After reducing PFT7/PFT9 vmax, PFT10's relative uptake share may increase significantly. If PFT10 vmax is not adjusted (currently vmax_p_10=5e-11 at lower bound in Case #322), PFT10 may still be excluded even with lower PFT7/9 competition. Consider a modest PFT10 vmax increase alongside PFT7/9 vmax reductions.
- L2FR CHANGE EFFECT ON P DEMAND: Changing l2fr_ini values affects root biomass (fnrt_C), which directly changes ECA P demand (demand = vmax × fnrt_C). Reducing l2fr_ini_9 from 18.31 to 5.0 reduces PFT9 root biomass by ~64%, directly reducing PFT9 P demand by ~64%. This is a BENEFICIAL side effect — the l2fr correction provides P demand relief without requiring vmax changes for PFT9.
